Order of the Minister · 20 October 2026 · No. 141

On the licensing of the sources of antimony ore

in force from 1 January 2027

1. A holder of a processing permit shall accept antimony ore only from the holder of a mining lease, with a delivery note naming the lease number, the mine and the weight.

2. Delivery notes shall be kept for five years and shown to the Chatkal Placer Mining Inspectorate on request.

3. This order takes effect on 1 January 2027.
