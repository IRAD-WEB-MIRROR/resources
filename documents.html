Documents and legal acts

Laws, regulations and orders of the Minister on mining, oil and gas, forests, protected areas and plant quarantine.

Order of the Minister · 20 October 2026 · No. 141

On the licensing of the sources of antimony ore

in force from 1 January 2027

From 1 January 2027 ore may be accepted only from licensed mines, with a delivery note naming the licence.

Order of the Minister · 14 March 2018 · No. 37

On the tailings-safety conditions of mining leases

in force

Monitoring of dams and pipelines, a reserve of capacity and quarterly reports, written into the leases of the Sary-Bulak mines and the Chatkal works.

Order of the Minister · 30 January 2018 · No. 12

On the royalty schedule for stibnite concentrate

in force

Revised schedule under the Hydrocarbon and Mining Code; royalties are deducted from export proceeds in escrow.

Act · 1 July 1997 · No. 97-14

Plant Quarantine Act

in force

Phytosanitary certificates, checks at the crossings, quarantine pests, the powers of the Inspectorate.

Regulation · 11 April 1996 · No. 3

Regulation on Besh-Tash National Park and its Ecological Conservation Area

in force

Boundaries, the core and the buffer zone, permits for trekking, riding and grazing, the ranger service.

Code · 30 June 1995 · 1995

Hydrocarbon and Mining Code

in force

Leases, concessions and permits, royalties, the road-fund chapter. Full text kept by the Mejlis.
