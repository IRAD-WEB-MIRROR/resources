Leadership

The Ministry of Natural Resources is headed by the Minister, appointed by the Governor, with a First Deputy Minister and a Deputy Minister. The Forestry and Protected Areas Service and the Plant Quarantine Inspectorate are headed by their own chiefs.

Chingiz Asanov

Minister of Natural Resources · since 2016

Chingiz Asanov was born in 1963. He graduated as a mining engineer from the Frunze Polytechnic Institute and went to work at the Sary-Bulak Antimony Works in 1985, becoming its chief engineer in 2001.

He was Deputy Minister of Natural Resources from 2009 and has been Minister since 2016. He introduced the revised stibnite royalty schedule and the tailings-safety conditions of the mining leases in 2018.

Personal reception

Reception of citizens: second Wednesday of the month, 14:00–17:00, by appointment (+996-39-2-21-31-00).

Deputies and officials

Almazbek Kasymaliev

First Deputy Minister · since 2017

Born in 1968 in Sary-Bulak. Geologist (Kyrgyz State University, 1990); worked for the territorial geological survey and the licensing department of the ministry, which he headed from 2007. Responsible for mining and oil licences and the register of deposits.

Irina Litvinenko

Deputy Minister · since 2021

Born in 1975 in Talas. Hydrologist; worked at the Hydrometeorological Service and from 2012 in the ministry's environmental department. Responsible for tailings and mine safety, water protection and the public hearings on permits.

Asylbek Toktomushev

Director, Forestry and Protected Areas Service · since 2014

Born in 1966. Forester; ranger and later head of the Besh-Tash Ranger Headquarters (1996–2014).

Dr Nazira Baigazieva

Chief State Plant Quarantine Inspector · since 2019

Born in 1972. Plant protection specialist, candidate of agricultural sciences.
