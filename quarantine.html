Plant Quarantine Inspectorate

The Plant Quarantine Inspectorate of the ministry's crop production department checks seed, plants, produce and wood brought into the Zone, issues phytosanitary certificates for exports, and keeps quarantine pests out of the Talas, Chu and Naryn valleys. It works with the Customs Service at the crossings and with the State Veterinary Inspectorate of the Ministry of Agriculture and Water Resources.

What is checked

GoodsRequirement
Seed potatoescertificate of the exporting country, laboratory check for nematodes and wart disease
Grain for sowingcertificate; samples to the laboratory; 10-day hold at the bonded yard
Fruit and vegetables for foodvisual check at the crossing; certificate for consignments over 500 kg
Seedlings, cuttings and nursery stockpermit before import; quarantine planting for one season
Wood packaging and timbermark of heat treatment; bark-free
