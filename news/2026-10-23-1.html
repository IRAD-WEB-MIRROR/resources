Ministry at the opening of Ilbirs Station

A representative of the ministry attended the opening of Ilbirs Station at Uuk-Bulak. The Forestry and Protected Areas Service will share its records of snow leopard sightings from the Chatkal Wildlife Protection Post with the station.

Press service of the Ministry of Natural Resources
