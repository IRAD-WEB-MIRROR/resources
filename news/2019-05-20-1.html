Fine for the tailings pipeline break

The ministry has fined the Sary-Bulak Antimony Works 3.4 million som for the break of its tailings pipeline in April, when slurry reached the Sary-Bulak stream. The Works has replaced the damaged section and installed pressure alarms.

Press service of the Ministry of Natural Resources
