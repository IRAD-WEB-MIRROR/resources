First environmental report of the Combine received

The ministry has received the first published environmental report of the Iradi Antimony and Rare Metals Combine, covering the tailings facilities at Sary-Bulak and Chatkal. The ministry's comments will be sent to the Combine within sixty days.

Press service of the Ministry of Natural Resources
