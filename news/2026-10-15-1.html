Besh-Tash season closes

The trekking season in Besh-Tash National Park closed on 15 October. The Ranger Headquarters at Kök-Oi gave 2,380 day permits this year, against 2,110 in 2025. Winter grazing permits for the buffer zone are given at the Headquarters on Mondays.
