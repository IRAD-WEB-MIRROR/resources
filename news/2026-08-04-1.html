Fire danger in the spruce forests

The Forestry and Protected Areas Service has declared the highest fire danger in the spruce forests of the Talas Alatau until further notice. Fires may be made only at the marked sites in the buffer zone of Besh-Tash. Report a fire on +996-39-2-21-31-99.

Press service of the Ministry of Natural Resources
