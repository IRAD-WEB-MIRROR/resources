Hearing on the Chatkal mercury unit set for 26 November

The public hearing on the application of the Chatkal Chemical Smelting Complex for a permit to restart its mercury recovery unit, idle since 2019, will be held at the Rudnik house of culture on 26 November at 15:00. Deputy Minister Irina Litvinenko will chair it. Written comments are received until 10 December.

Press service of the Ministry of Natural Resources
